Type it, say it, photograph it or write it by hand, and OpenTranslate translates it. It's a full
alternative to Google Translate, with one big difference: **you decide where your words go.** Most of the
time they don't go anywhere at all.

## Everything you need, in one app

- **Text.** Translates while you type. Hear it read aloud, and tap again to hear it slower for practising.
  History and saved phrases are always at hand.
- **Camera.** Point at a menu, a sign or a letter and read it in your language. Works with photos from your
  gallery too.
- **Conversation.** Two people, two languages, one phone. Talk in turns and each of you hears the other in
  your own language.
- **Handwriting.** Draw a word or a character you can't type and translate it.
- **Share to OpenTranslate.** Share text, a link or a photo from any app and it's translated right away.

## Private by default

Translation apps usually send everything you type to a server. OpenTranslate can translate **on your
device**: download a language once, pick the on-device options, and nothing you type, say or photograph
leaves your phone. No account, no tracking, no ads.

## Works without internet

On a plane, abroad without a data plan, deep in the countryside: download the languages you need before you
go, and text, camera and read aloud keep working offline. Around 58 languages are available.

## Bring your own AI, or none at all

Want the best possible translation? Connect the AI service you already use, or a model running on your
own computer, and OpenTranslate uses it. Prefer to stay fully offline? Run a small AI model right in your
browser. Or don't use AI at all. Your choice, and you can change it at any time.

Every option in Settings tells you plainly whether it's **private** or **sends to** a service, whether it
**works offline**, and how big the download is. If one option can't answer, the next one steps in, and
the app tells you why.

## Made for everyone

- Works on phones, tablets and computers, in any modern browser.
- Install it to your home screen like any app (**Add to Home screen** or **Install**).
- Designed for accessibility: large touch targets, full keyboard use, screen reader labels, light and dark
  mode, and text that stays readable when you zoom.
